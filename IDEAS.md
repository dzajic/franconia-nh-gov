# Ideas

Add an entry under the group it belongs to: a title, one or two lines, a status (`parked`, `exploring`, or `in PR #n`), and the date added. Update the status when work starts, and remove or rewrite the entry once it ships or is dropped.

PRINCIPLES.md is the yardstick for what belongs here. It is not on `main` yet; the draft in [PR #16](https://github.com/dzajic/franconia-nh-gov/pull/16) is.

## Resident input

### Email suggestions to draft PRs

The site’s “Contact by email” option goes to the page owner’s own inbox. The owner screens it for spam and abuse and forwards real suggestions, with optional instructions, to a bot address; only forwards from approved owners trigger an AI-drafted pull request that a person reviews and merges.
