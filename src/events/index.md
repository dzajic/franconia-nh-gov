Have you ever wondered what happens to that aluminum can once you throw it in the recycling bin? What makes recycling different in New Hampshire compared to our neighboring states? Join NH Recycles to get answers at the Recycling 101 presentation and Q&A. Bring friends and neighbors to learn what steps we can all take to make a difference. Thanks to support from an EPA grant, this event is free; registration is requested.

Thank you to the Tri-Town Transfer Station Committee — the towns of Easton, Sugarhill, and Franconia — and the Ammonoosuc Conservation Trust for inviting NH Recycles.
